EduAItor is an AI-powered School Operating System that helps schools run daily operations from one app — for administrators, teachers, staff, and parents.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Mark and track class attendance in real time
• Manage students, staff, and parent contacts
• Collect and follow up on school fees
• Share homework, circulars, and notices
• View attendance trends and school reports
• Role-based access for Principal, Teacher, Staff, and Parent

BUILT FOR SCHOOLS
EduAItor brings admissions, academics, attendance, fees, communication, and analytics into one secure platform so your team spends less time on paperwork and more time on teaching.

WHO IT’S FOR
• School admins & principals — dashboards, reports, and control
• Teachers — attendance, homework, and class tools
• Parents — child’s attendance, homework, fees, and school updates
